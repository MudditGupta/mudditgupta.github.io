Founder playbooktap to open
01Validate before you write a line of code+
Talk to 10 people with the problem. Ask what they do today and what it costs them. Don't pitch. If nobody has hacked together a workaround, the pain probably isn't real.
02Your MVP is too big. Cut it in half.+
Write down the one job a user hires your product for. Every feature that doesn't serve that job goes to a "v2" list. Admin panels, dashboards and settings can wait.
03Devs build what you write, not what you mean+
For every feature: user story, acceptance criteria, and the edge cases (empty state, errors, permissions). Vague specs are why projects go over budget and timeline.
04Track 3 numbers, not 30+
Activation (did they reach the "aha"?), retention (did they come back?), and conversion to paid. Everything else is a vanity metric at the early stage.
05Map the workflow before the screens+
Draw how the user's work moves today, step by step. The product should remove steps from that flow. Designing screens first gives you a pretty app nobody needs.
