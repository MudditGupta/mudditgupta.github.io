How I work

From a fuzzy problem to a shippable solution.

I bring business understanding and technical context into the same conversation — so teams can make decisions with fewer assumptions.

Step 01

Understand the problem

Stakeholder conversations, business goals, user needs, current-state workflows and gap analysis.

Step 02

Define what matters

Scope, MVP boundaries, functional requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria and priorities.

Step 03

Align the team

Backlog refinement, engineering clarification, dependencies, risks and change management.

Step 04

Validate delivery

QA/UAT scenarios, business validation, release readiness and feedback loops.
